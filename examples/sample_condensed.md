## Atrium — before morning shift — present: Mott, Peg (passing)

The PC finds Mott at his usual table, watching the door over an open ledger, and sits.

**Mott:** "The registry's been amended. Page forty-one. I didn't amend it."

The PC asks who else has write access. Mott closes the ledger.

**Mott:** "Three people. Me, the Chief Counter, and whoever's been dead long enough that nobody changed the password."

Peg drops off two mugs unasked.

**Peg:** "You two look like a funeral that lost its body."

**Mott:** "Page forty-one, kid. Read it before Thursday."

The PC reads page forty-one at the table: the columbarium allocation list. One niche reassigned, the previous occupant's name struck through in recent ink — a name the PC knows. The PC now carries Mott's ledger.
